# Seraphim project instructions

- The repository root is the Unity project root. Keep Unity 6000.3.28f1 and URP 17.3.0 pinned until an upgrade is requested or needed for a documented fix.
- Windows x64 is the current release target. Maintain the overhead camera, WASD and clicking movement, six-winged angel identity, and no blood/gore direction.
- Use C# for gameplay and editor automation. Use the existing build tools instead of requiring manual scene wiring for routine changes.
- `Tools/Build.ps1` includes scene/shader/dependency validation. After gameplay changes, also run `Tools/Smoke-Test.ps1` against the exported Windows player and inspect relevant screenshots. Report actual results and limitations.
- Preserve asset `.meta` files and serialized settings. Existing settings assets override C# field initializer defaults; change the relevant asset when changing an existing project's tuning.
- Never commit Library, Temp, Logs, UserSettings, Builds, installer caches, tokens, license files, or machine-local tool configuration.
- This is a public repository. CC0 assets and source notices may be checked in. Keep restricted Asset Store content in the ignored `Assets/ThirdParty/AssetStore/` directory; inspect licenses before adding other third-party content.
- Record asset origin, license, and checksums when adding downloadable dependencies. Keep the project buildable from a clean checkout plus the pinned editor and an active local Unity license.
- Keep movement, abilities, presentation, and content definitions separate. Expand systems through small playable milestones rather than implementing every planned RPG feature at once.
- The HUD uses native Unity UI in camera space. Keep Life left, Spirit right, numbered skill slots in the center, and HUD clicks isolated from world movement. Preserve the 30-second Word of God cooldown and restore temporary character/camera effects after casting.
- Word of God follows the player and pulses for 15 active seconds after charging. Keep all five combat powers playable, enemy spawning bounded, and damage/status effects and overhead health bars consistent when pooled demons are reused.
- Keep the ten-wave cathedral scenario, score, bounded restoration drops, and north-up minimap coherent. Navigation, spawn bounds, and map landscape share cathedral dimensions and obstacle definitions. Defeat retries the current quota; victory stops spawning and permits replay.
- Support keyboard/mouse and gamepads concurrently. Skill order is Radiance, Lance, Chain, Inferno, Word of God. Controller face-button positions are South primary, West spears, East lightning, North fire; D-pad Up activates slot 5. Keep analog steering, deadzones, hot-plug cleanup, shared cooldowns, and controller prompts coherent. Input System 1.20.1 and Both input back ends are pinned.
- Difficulty now ramps to 64 demons in wave ten, with a 24-enemy living cap and four concurrent special attacks. Preserve readable fixed telegraphs, interruptible windups/charges, navigable charge paths, and complete threat/stat cleanup when demons die or return to a pool.
- Main-menu showcase uses a separate 96-enemy cap and autonomous movement/powers. Starting must clear demonstration state and restore campaign camera, resources, cooldowns and input. In-game settings pause combat; menu settings keep the showcase running. Preserve simultaneous keyboard/mouse/controller play after closing UI.
- Showcase navigation must sustain full-speed runs between distant reachable destinations, with casts performed while moving. Restore campaign speed and crowd avoidance on exit. Front-end checks measure actual travel over time, including a 90-second focused soak; do not replace this with requested movement speed or a moving target the hero can catch.
- Keep independent persistent Music and Sound Effects volumes and the original audio fallback. The owner-authorized Track Realm recording stays in ignored LocalAudio/ and local Windows exports, with origin and checksum receipts; do not publish its raw audio. Stream the long OGG from disk. Front-end smoke checks must restore saved volume preferences.
- After ten waves, queue Vorath and require his defeat for victory. Boss Trial uses the same encounter. Preserve all five powers against his shared enemy health pool, three phases, locked attack geometry, meteor fire, wall-blocked projectiles, nova safe zones and directional scythe dodge routes. Boss death/retry/menu/replay must clear every hazard and temporary camera setting; an ordinary spawn must never reuse the dedicated boss. Keep boss hazards bounded to 18, separate from ordinary demon attacks, and retry the boss without repeating ten waves.
- Boss damage must stop at 65% and 30% health so burst damage cannot skip phase transitions. Defer spawning until Update, outside damage iteration. Transition wards are temporary and visibly labeled. Phase two summons up to six demons; phase three animates 1.55x boss growth and 1.35x summon growth, with a ten-summon cap. Summons attack, score and drop normal resources; clear them on boss death, retry, menu or replay and reset all growth/flags when pooled enemies are reused.
- Life HUD, drops, collection effects and minimap resources use the shared gold palette; Spirit uses silver throughout. Preserve Life left and Spirit right.
- Character art now uses CC BY 4.0 FBX/PNG derivatives under Assets/ThirdParty/CCBY/Characters: FallenAngel, four demon classes, and the Infernal Wyrm dragon knight for Vorath. Preserve authored PBR material slots, six angel wings, humanoid player retargeting, skeletal enemy motion and one two-level LODGroup per demon/boss. CharacterContentBuilder regenerates prefabs/catalog references and verifies source receipts during builds. Keep original ZIPs and Blender in ignored .cache; use Tools/Prepare-CharacterModels.ps1 for source preparation and retain attribution in Windows exports. The fourth DemonKind is a rare RiftScourger with an interruptible fixed rift warning; do not break caps or the existing three enemy numeric IDs.
- The local 0.11 presentation bank maps 23 sound cues and 24 effects from four licensed Asset Store packs. DownloadedPresentationBuilder moves default import roots under ignored Assets/ThirdParty/AssetStore/ with GUIDs intact and regenerates the private Resources bank. Never commit the packs, adapted prefabs/materials or bank. Preserve the original clean-checkout/runtime fallback, independent channel volumes, 20 audio voices, and SanctuaryPresentation's 32 active/96 retained effect limits; imported visuals must never apply damage or alter fixed warnings. Include the imported particle shader in build and runtime shader checks. Record original import provenance in Tools/presentation-sources.json.
- Vorath now uses InfernalKnightAnimation with an articulated fitted rig, continuous skin-weight blends, a separate rigid sword bound to Hand.R, a BladeTip joint and Cape joint. Keep idle/walk/cast, the 1.05-second sword windup, sweep/recovery, transformation gestures and blade-trail cleanup synchronized with the existing encounter. Validate hand/shoulder movement and rigid blade length in the Windows player; reset every pose/trail on reuse.
